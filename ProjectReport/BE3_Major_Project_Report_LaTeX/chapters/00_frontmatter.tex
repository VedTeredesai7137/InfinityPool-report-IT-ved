%==================== ABSTRACT ====================
\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\vspace{-9pt}
\noindent Business teams in HR, marketing and podcast production spend much of their time on repetitive work such as screening resumes, rewriting posts for each channel and researching guests. Existing tools mostly store records or match keywords, and their AI features give opaque results that are difficult to explain or audit. Our project aims to build an integrated agentic AI platform in which a workflow router coordinates specialised LLM agents for recruitment, marketing, podcast guest research and enterprise integrations. It combines retrieval-augmented generation with rubric-based, evidence-backed scoring and secure connectors to Zoho, Workday and Greenhouse-style systems. The system generates rubrics from job descriptions, screens and interviews candidates, drafts channel-specific content and prepares source-backed guest briefings. By placing human approvals, PII protection and audit logs around consequential actions, the platform helps organisations automate routine work while keeping people in control of final decisions.
